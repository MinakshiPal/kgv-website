Custom Jewelry Manufacturing

Get your own design crafted in gold or silver at Kasturchand Gendalal Vagrecha

We Create Jewelry As Per Your Design

At Kasturchand Gendalal Vagrecha, we don't just sell ready-made jewelry — we manufacture it. Share your idea, sketch, photo or requirement, and our skilled artisans will craft it into beautiful gold or silver jewelry.

What We Can Make for You

  • Custom Bangles, Bajuband, Kangans
  • Custom Earrings (Jhumkas, studs, traditional designs)
  • Silver Payal and Kada with your preferred patterns
  • Complete wedding jewelry sets (bridal sets, family sets)
  • Bulk orders for retailers, events and festivals

How It Works

  1. Share Your Idea: Tell us your design, occasion and budget.
  2. Discussion: We discuss design, weight, purity and approximate cost.
  3. Manufacturing: Our karigars start crafting your jewelry.
  4. Delivery: Once ready, you can collect it from our shop or arrange delivery.

Why Choose Us for Custom Jewelry

  • In-house manufacturing unit with experienced artisans
  • Flexibility in design, weight and purity
  • Competitive making charges
  • Transparent process and pricing
