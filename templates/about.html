About Kasturchand Gendalal Vagrecha

Gold & Silver Jewelry Manufacturers & Retailers in Khachrod, Ujjain

Our Story

Kasturchand Gendalal Vagrecha (KGV) has been a trusted name in Khachrod, Ujjain for many years. We specialize in manufacturing and retailing gold and silver jewelry, including Bangles, Bajuband, Kangans, Earrings, Silver Payal, Kada and other traditional ornaments.

Our journey began with a vision to provide high-quality, beautifully crafted jewelry at fair prices. Over the years, we have built strong relationships with our customers through honesty, craftsmanship and reliable service.

Our Work: Manufacturing & Retail

At our workshop, skilled artisans craft jewelry by hand, combining traditional techniques with modern finishing. We handle:

  • Designing and manufacturing of gold jewelry
  • Designing and manufacturing of silver jewelry
  • Retail sale of finished ornaments
  • Custom orders for weddings, festivals and bulk requirements

Our Product Range

  • Gold Jewelry: Traditional ornaments, wedding sets, custom designs
  • Silver Jewelry: Bangles, Payal, Kada, Earrings and more
  • Special Items: Bangles, Bajuband, Kangans, Earrings, Payal, Kada, etc.

Our Values

  • Quality: We use quality materials and ensure fine finishing.
  • Purity: Honest disclosure of gold/silver purity.
  • Trust: Transparent pricing and long-term relationships.
  • Craftsmanship: Skilled karigars with years of experience.
